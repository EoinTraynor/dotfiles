# Global Engineering Guidelines

- **Concise Communication**: Provide direct, actionable explanations and avoid unnecessary fluff.
- **Code Integrity**: Preserve existing comments, formatting standards, and docstrings unless explicitly refactoring.
- **Security & Secrets**: Never write, log, or commit API keys, authentication tokens, or credentials to files or git.
- **Git Hygiene**: Follow conventional commits (`feat:`, `fix:`, `chore:`, `refactor:`, `docs:`), and ensure diffs are minimal and focused.
